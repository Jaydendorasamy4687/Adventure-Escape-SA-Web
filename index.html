Outdoor Experience Catalog

1. Ziplining Canopy Tour

High-speed zip slides traversing lush mountain valleys and river canyons.

  • Duration: 2 Hours
  • Difficulty Level: Moderate
  • Base Price: R400 per participant
  • Gear Provided: Full Body Harness, Safety Helmet, Gloves

2. River Kayaking

Guided paddling along pristine river courses suitable for both beginners and experts.

  • Duration: 3 Hours
  • Difficulty Level: Easy to Moderate
  • Base Price: R350 per participant
  • Gear Provided: Life Jacket, Paddle, Waterproof Container

3. Rock Climbing

Conquer natural granite cliff faces with secure top-rope safety systems.

  • Duration: 4 Hours
  • Difficulty Level: Advanced
  • Base Price: R500 per participant
  • Gear Provided: Harness, Climbing Shoes, Helmet, Chalk Bag
